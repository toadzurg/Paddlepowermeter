Force sensor

Tare with the blade unloaded. To calibrate, clamp the shaft at the top hand, power face up, and tilt it so the blade face is level (on a bent shaft that's the bend angle, e.g. 12°). Rest a known weight on the blade's centre and enter its mass.

Pair away from other boats. To calibrate, start, paddle a measured distance on still water, then enter the distance.
